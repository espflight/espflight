# ESPFlight Brand Policy

The ESPFlight name, logo, visual identity, and other ESPFlight brand assets identify the ESPFlight platform and are separate from the open-source and open-hardware licenses applied to ESPFlight technology.

Open-source, open-hardware, and documentation licenses do not grant permission to use ESPFlight branding except as expressly allowed by this policy.

## Permitted References

You may make accurate factual references to ESPFlight when describing compatibility, technical origin, or use of the platform.

Examples include:

* “Based on ESPFlight”
* “Built using ESPFlight”
* “Compatible with ESPFlight”
* “Uses ESPFlight Firmware”
* “Designed for use with the ESPFlight Application”

Such references must not imply that an independent project or product is officially produced, approved, certified, sponsored, partnered with, or endorsed by ESPFlight.

## Independent Products

Makers, developers, engineers, independent teams, and businesses may create hardware, kits, products, and projects based on ESPFlight technology subject to the applicable software, hardware, and documentation licenses.

Independent products must use their own names and branding.

ESPFlight must not be used as the primary product name or brand in a manner that could cause users to believe that an independent product is an official ESPFlight product.

## ESPFlight Logo

The ESPFlight logo and other official ESPFlight brand graphics may not be used as branding on independent products, hardware, packaging, applications, websites, advertising, or promotional materials without prior authorization.

This restriction does not prevent ordinary factual references to the ESPFlight name as described in this policy.

## Prohibited Uses

Without prior authorization, you may not:

* present an independent product as an “Official ESPFlight” product;
* use names such as “ESPFlight Official Kit” or similar wording;
* claim that a project or product is certified, approved, sponsored, partnered with, or endorsed by ESPFlight when it is not;
* use the ESPFlight logo as the primary branding of an independent product;
* use ESPFlight branding in a way that may confuse users about the source, ownership, or official status of a product;
* register or use names, logos, domains, accounts, or branding that misleadingly impersonates ESPFlight;
* use ESPFlight branding in a deceptive or misleading manner.

## Clear Attribution

Independent products may use wording such as:

**“Based on the ESPFlight platform.”**

or:

**“Compatible with the ESPFlight platform.”**

Where there is a reasonable possibility of confusion, additional clarification should be provided that the product is independently developed and is not an official ESPFlight product.

## Modified Projects

Projects based on modified ESPFlight hardware or firmware may accurately state their technical relationship to ESPFlight.

However, modifications should not be represented as official ESPFlight versions unless they have been explicitly released or approved as such by ESPFlight.

## Open Technology, Independent Brands

ESPFlight encourages learning, experimentation, modification, development, research, and the creation of independent projects and products based on its open technologies.

The purpose of this Brand Policy is not to restrict those activities.

Its purpose is to maintain a clear distinction between the ESPFlight platform itself and independently developed products built using ESPFlight technology.
