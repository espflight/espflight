# ESPFlight — Core Identity

## Official Definition

ESPFlight is an open platform for learning, building, experimenting with, and developing ESP-based drones.

It brings together open-source flight firmware, open hardware reference designs, documentation, and the ESPFlight Application to provide a practical foundation for creating and exploring ESP-based drone systems.

## Purpose

ESPFlight exists to make drone technology easier to understand, build, modify, and develop.

Instead of treating a drone as a closed finished product, ESPFlight provides accessible building blocks that allow users to explore how the system works, experiment with it, customize it, and create something of their own.

## Who ESPFlight Is For

ESPFlight is primarily designed for:

* Enthusiasts
* Students and university students
* Teachers, instructors, and educators
* Makers
* Developers
* Engineers

It can be used for learning, experimentation, education, prototyping, development, research, and independent projects.

## What ESPFlight Provides

ESPFlight provides a common foundation consisting of:

* Open-source flight firmware
* Open hardware reference designs
* ESPFlight Application for configuration and control
* Documentation and build guides
* A foundation for custom ESP-based drone development

The components are designed to work together while remaining flexible enough for users to modify, extend, and adapt their own projects.

## Open by Design

ESPFlight is intended to be a foundation rather than a closed ecosystem.

Users can study the platform, build the reference hardware, modify hardware designs, change or extend the firmware, experiment with different configurations, develop new features, and create their own projects around ESPFlight.

Subject to the applicable licenses, Makers, developers, engineers, and independent teams may also build their own kits and products based on ESPFlight and distribute or sell them under their own independent brands.

## What ESPFlight Is Not

ESPFlight is not a commercial drone kit brand.

There is no official ESPFlight hardware kit that users are required to purchase.

Hardware published by ESPFlight is provided as a reference design that can be built, studied, modified, or used as the foundation for independent projects and products.

ESPFlight itself focuses on the platform, firmware, hardware references, application, documentation, and ecosystem.

## Philosophy

ESPFlight aims to reduce the gap between learning about drone technology and actually building and developing it.

The platform encourages users not only to use a working system, but also to understand it, experiment with it, modify it, and eventually create their own solutions.

ESPFlight is built around four simple ideas:

**Learn it. Build it. Change it. Create your own.**
